## 🎯 AI Article Generation Guidelines

### **When Creating Articles, Please:**

1. **Focus on Target Keywords**: Naturally integrate Istanbul/Turkey and web design keywords
2. **Provide Real Value**: Don't just promote services; offer genuine insights
3. **Include Local Context**: Reference Istanbul business scene, Turkey market trends
4. **Use Professional Tone**: Expert but accessible writing style
5. **Add Actionable Tips**: Give readers something they can implement
6. **Include CTAs**: End with relevant call-to-action for services
7. **Optimize for SEO**: Use keywords in headlines, subheadings, meta descriptions
8. **Make it Scannable**: Use bullet points, numbered lists, clear structure
9. **Reference Portfolio**: When relevant, tie content back to actual project experience
10. **Stay Current**: Focus on current trends and technologies in web design

### **Article Length Guidelines**

- **Blog Posts**: 1,500-2,500 words for SEO authority
- **Tutorials**: 2,000-3,000 words with step-by-step instructions
- **Case Studies**: 1,000-1,500 words with visuals and results
- **Trend Articles**: 1,200-2,000 words with multiple examples
