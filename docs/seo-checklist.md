## Review Solicitation

Google prioritizes **recent** reviews over lifetime totals. Aim for 1-2 reviews per month.

Contact past clients and ask politely:
> Hi [Name], I hope [project] is going well! If you've been happy with the work, would you mind leaving a quick review on my Google Business page? It really helps other businesses find me. Here's the link: [your GBP review link]
