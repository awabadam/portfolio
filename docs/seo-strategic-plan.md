# SEO & Growth Strategic Plan
## Awab Design | Istanbul Web Designer

**Created:** April 2026
**Goal:** Improve CTR and impressions on Google Search
**Approach:** Go narrow first (medical clinic niche), then expand

---

## Strategic Overview

### The Core Insight
The biggest opportunity isn't competing for "web design Istanbul" (too competitive). It's owning a specific niche where no quality competitor exists: **medical clinics needing Next.js + multilingual + AI chatbots**.

### The Four Phases
| Phase | Timeframe | Focus | Goal |
|-------|-----------|-------|------|
| **1. Foundation Fixes** | Week 1 | Quick wins on existing pages | Immediate CTR + local impressions |
| **2. Clinic Niche Domination** | Week 2-3 | New landing page + case studies | Own the medical tourism keyword space |
| **3. Turkish Expansion** | Week 4-6 | Localize for the local market | Capture untapped Turkish search volume |
| **4. Content Engine** | Month 2-3 | Blog + ongoing publishing | Long-term organic traffic compound |

---

## Phase 1 — Foundation Fixes (Week 1)

**Goal:** Cheap, fast wins that compound with everything else.

### Tasks
1. Audit & rewrite meta titles/descriptions on existing pages with target keywords
2. Google Business Profile deep optimization (category, services, posts, photos)
3. Add FAQ schema to service pages (answering top 25 client questions)

## Phase 1 — Detailed Action Plan

### 1.1 Meta Tags Audit & Rewrite

**Goal:** Better CTR from search results by matching search intent and including target keywords.

**Pages to update (priority order):**
1. Homepage (`/`)
2. Services page (`/services`)
3. About page (`/about`)
4. Contact page (`/contact`)
5. Individual service pages (if any)

**Current homepage meta:**
- Title: "Awab Design | Web Designer & Developer | Istanbul"
- Description: "Professional web designer and developer in Istanbul. Specializing in modern, conversion-focused websites using Next.js, React, and Tailwind CSS."

**Proposed homepage meta (optimized):**
- Title: "Web Designer Istanbul | Next.js & Multilingual Sites | Awab Design"
- Description: "Freelance web designer in Istanbul building fast, multilingual websites with Next.js. Specializing in clinics, agencies & e-commerce. Starting at $500."

**Rules for rewriting:**
- Title: 50-60 chars, primary keyword early, brand last
- Description: 150-160 chars, include keyword + differentiator + CTA
- Never keyword-stuff; write for humans first
- Each page must have unique meta
- Include power words: "freelance", "Istanbul", "multilingual", "Next.js"

### 1.3 FAQ Schema on Service Pages

**Goal:** Get rich snippets in search results (higher CTR).

**Top FAQs to answer:**
1. How much does a website cost in Istanbul?
2. How long does it take to build a website?
3. Do you build custom websites or use templates?
4. Do you offer SEO?
5. Will I own the website after launch?
6. Do you provide post-launch support?
7. Do you build multilingual websites?
8. What technology do you use?
9. Do you work with clients outside Istanbul?
10. What's included in your pricing?

**Implementation:** Create a `<FAQSchema>` component that outputs JSON-LD `FAQPage` structured data. Add to homepage and services page.

### 1.4 Internal Linking Audit

**Goal:** Distribute page authority and help Google understand site structure.

**Action items:**
- [ ] Audit all existing pages for linking opportunities
- [ ] Add contextual links from homepage to services, projects, about
- [ ] Link project case studies back to relevant service pages
- [ ] Add a "Related Services" section to service pages
- [ ] Ensure footer has all key pages
- [ ] Use keyword-rich anchor text (not "click here")

### 1.5 Transparent Pricing Page

**Goal:** Kill the #1 sales objection AND rank for "web design prices istanbul".

**Page structure:**
- URL: `/pricing`
- Hero: "Transparent Pricing. No Hidden Fees."
- Three clear packages:
  - **Starter**: $500 — Landing page, 1 language, basic SEO
  - **Business**: $1,500 — 5-page site, 2 languages, full SEO, contact forms
  - **Premium**: $3,000+ — Custom build, 4 languages, AI chatbot, CMS
- What's included / not included comparison table
- FAQ section: "What's not included?", "Payment plans?", "Can I upgrade later?"
- CTA: Free consultation booking
- Trust signals: testimonials, past project logos

**Target keywords:**
- "web design prices istanbul"
- "freelance web designer rates istanbul"
- "web tasarim fiyatlari 2026"
- "how much does a website cost in istanbul"
